The scale: Elon Musk
$432B
Net worth · 31 Dec 2024 · Bloomberg ↗

Why Elon Musk? At $432B, his 2024 year-end net worth exceeded the entire GDP of over 150 countries. Using a single person's fortune as a unit of national output makes the scale of the world economy visceral — when the US clocks 68× and Tuvalu a fraction of 1×, the breadth of human economic activity becomes impossible to ignore.
