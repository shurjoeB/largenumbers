About this project

Large numbers
stop making sense.

We come across very large numbers every day: Nvidia crosses $5 trillion in market cap, FIIs take out $50 million from the Indian markets, the BJP spends ₹3,335 crore on elections in 2024–25, so on and so forth. After a certain threshold we can't comprehend these very large numbers — the FII outflow gets lumped in the same group of numbers as Nvidia's market cap in our head.

This space is an effort to contextualise these very large numbers in relatable terms and make sense of the numbers which we struggle with when represented in their absolute form.
