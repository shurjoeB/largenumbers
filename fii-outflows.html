Foreign Portfolio Investor net investment in India, 2000–2026. Each bar is one month (or year). Green = inflow, red = outflow. 2026 data through September.

The scale: Apollo Hospitals
₹1,02,665 cr
Market cap · 2 January 2026 (NSE: APOLLOHOSP)
Used as a fixed benchmark across all years.

Why Apollo? It is India's largest standalone hospital chain by market capitalisation — a number most readers intuitively grasp. At ~₹1 lakh crore, it is large enough to feel substantial, yet small enough that even large FPI flow events run into meaningful multiples, making the comparison easy to feel.

Apollo benchmark: ₹1,02,665 crore = NSE closing price ₹7,129.50 × ~14.4 crore shares on 2 January 2026. Fixed across all years for comparability — this is a contemporaneous yardstick, not historical Apollo size. % NSE market cap: FPI net flow as a share of total NSE listed-company market cap for that year (December year-end).
