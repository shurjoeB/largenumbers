Why Swachh Bharat? The SBM is India's most widely-known public welfare scheme — a ₹12,192 crore annual budget that most citizens can intuitively grasp. Measuring scams against it answers the question everyone actually wants to ask: how many years of India's flagship sanitation programme could each scandal have funded?

How to read

Notes & methodology

  • Each India map equals India's Swachh Bharat Mission budget for FY 2025–26: ₹12,192 crore (Gramin ₹7,192 cr + Urban ₹5,000 cr). Full maps are 100% filled; partial maps are filled from the bottom up.
  • All amounts are inflation-adjusted to 2024 INR using India CPI Combined (Base 2012=100), from RBI Handbook Table 34. Pre-2012 values use a spliced CPI-IW series (±5–8% uncertainty).
  • Headline amounts represent different things — notional losses, allegations, investor liabilities, debt defaults. They are not additive and must not be summed.
  • Items marked [est] with a dashed silhouette border use a best-guess estimate where no single verified figure is publicly available.
  • An amount in a CAG report or FIR is not a proven loss. Acquittals, ongoing proceedings and appeals are noted in each item's detail.
  • Data last verified: October 2026.
